# Content Guide

## Tone

Professional, direct, sober and technical.

Avoid hype.

Use:

- applied AI;
- automation;
- RAG;
- agents;
- internal systems;
- technical documentation;
- static websites without backend for businesses, professionals and establishments.

Avoid:

- magic AI;
- fake seniority;
- fake metrics;
- fake clients;
- unverifiable guarantees;
- exaggerated model claims;
- inflated results.

## Core positioning

CDLAN designs practical systems that turn scattered processes into clear, maintainable workflows.

## Service blocks

Recommended public sections:

1. AI diagnosis and process review.
2. RAG and internal knowledge systems.
3. N8N automation and workflow design.
4. AI agents and assistants.
5. Static websites without backend for businesses and establishments.
6. Maintenance and continuous improvement.

## Claims policy

Every public claim must be either:

- directly true;
- verifiable;
- clearly marked as laboratory/internal;
- modest enough to be defensible.

Do not publish model versions, performance numbers, prices, tax claims or response times unless reviewed at publication time.
